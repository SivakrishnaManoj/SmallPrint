1. Unzip smallprint.zip, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder.
2. Pin Small Print, open any product, subscription or checkout page (for example an Amazon UK product), and click the Small Print icon to open the side panel.
3. On the first-run form press Skip (12 months, every kind of record); cards then stream in, each with its quote and source link.
